## `~/` whoami

```console
$ chawla --profile --fast
```

Hi, I'm **Sahaj Chawla**. I'm building **[ONEWAY](https://1way.dev)**, a workspace that shows AI builders the real cost and margin of every AI product they ship. Before this I co-founded three ventures in travel and B2B procurement, mostly on growth, operations and 0-to-1 execution. I build with AI tools every day and ship fast.

- 🧭 **Building [ONEWAY](https://1way.dev)**: one API, one balance, per-project AI spend and margin. Interactive preview is live; private beta is next.
- ⛺ Co-founded **Gulaab Jamoon → [Weekoff](https://weekoff.in/)** (2022–2026): a travel community with 100+ trips. I led growth and operations.
- 🚀 Co-founded the B2B procurement wing of the same group, now **[Keystone Commerce / Liwip](https://www.liwip.com/)** (2023–2026): bootstrapped to a ₹30L/month run-rate.
- 🧠 Built **[iLoveMD](https://ilovemd.online)**: a free, in-browser Markdown converter for LLM workflows, with a Chrome extension.
- 🏆 Winner (with my team), India Sustainability Startathon 2023. Represented India at ACIYLS 2023, Singapore.

> *Chai-paglu. Generalist at heart, bias to ship fast.*
